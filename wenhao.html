“Make something people want, then make it easy to pay for.”

— stuck on my monitor since 2023

Ship before you’re ready

The product teaches you what to build. Real users beat a perfect roadmap every time.

#productJun 02

The CTO is also the janitor

At a small company, leadership is mostly removing the rocks blocking everyone else’s path.

#leadershipMay 08

Boring tech, exciting product

Postgres + a monolith got me further than any fancy stack. Save novelty for the parts users actually feel.

#engineeringMay 24

Charge from day one

Free users give feedback; paying users give truth. Price early, even if it stings.

Write the changelog daily
